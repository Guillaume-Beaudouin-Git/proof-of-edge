Notes on the data

  • External balance adjustments (removed from the curve, counted outside P&L):
    • 2026-10-01: −$10.78. The first funded login was closed by a payout request after the flat close of 2026-09-30 (balance $10,010.78). A second funded login opened at $10,000.00 on 2026-10-01, same programme and limits. The series chains the two logins; the high-water mark of the first is carried into the second. The $10.78 left on the first login is counted as realised, outside the curve. The payout statement is not in this repository.
  • Fills that could not be paired (2). The EA logs the orders it sends and did not log these exits; their P&L is in the equity curve, not in the trade tables. They are also the two largest positions of the record:
    • 2026-09-07 16:20:16 US100 short 0.6 lots, notional $17,744.00, funded_orb_entry: entry without exit. Sized on the opening range of a US holiday session (Labor Day): $17.7k notional, about 1.7 times the account, the largest position of the record. The EA did not log the exit; the position is gone from the 2026-09-08 22:58 snapshot.
    • 2026-09-29 22:05:00 XAUUSD short 0.04 lots, notional $16,697.00, rebalance: entry without exit. Last trade of the gold sleeve, switched off on 2026-09-30: $16.7k notional, about 1.7 times the account. The EA did not log the exit (the book was flattened on 2026-09-30); this position accounts for most of the +$96.43 of 2026-09-30, the best day of the record.
  • Missing weekdays in the mark series: none.
  • Nightly reconciliation (real P&L against a replay of the model on the day's prices): 64 nights logged, 64 with a full model replay, 0 degraded (close-freeze fallback); 17 flagged above tolerance; largest residual $24.41; real +$72.35 vs model +$257.73 on the replayed days. Real and model differ by $185.38; −$156.43 of it sits in the ORB term (d_micro), on days where the live ORB fired and the daily replay saw no trade (2026-09-25, 2026-10-02 among others) or the reverse (2026-09-04); 2026-08-21 is an unexplained residual of the same size. Why the live ORB and the replay disagree on firing is open. Window: pnl_real is the terminal's equity at the day's close mark minus the 00:00 balance of the same day: pre-swap, and without the close-to-midnight interval, so it does not sum to the 00:00-to-00:00 P&L.
